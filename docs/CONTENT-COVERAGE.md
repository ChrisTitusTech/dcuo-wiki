# Conversion coverage audit

Checked the 12 initial guides against Firecrawl source captures on 2026-10-20.
See `data/coverage-audit.json` for source URLs, capture dates, source hashes,
section inventories and individual findings. This is a section-level audit,
not in-game verification or a guarantee of factual accuracy.

Powers now includes all 15 power sets, classic/Marketplace access, complexity,
role/stat guidance, Iconic and Artifact Powers, notes and all 16 source images.
Linked ability-list pages remain external source references until converted.
Existing section IDs and baseline text are unchanged, preserving live edits.

Prioritize these incomplete overview pages before another broad import:

1. Collections: six examples currently stand in for the collection directories.
2. Artifacts: known-artifact and upgrade/catalyst tables are missing.
3. Investigations: Gotham, Metropolis and Global directories are missing.
4. Feats: category directory and illustrated menu coverage are missing.

Equipment and the lighthouse need fuller references and artwork. The quest and
investigation guides generally retain objectives/routes but omit some dialogue,
combatants and gallery images. Weapon Mastery retains its full combo table.
These pages were audited, not expanded in this Powers change.

## Acceptance for future conversions

- Inventory every substantive source section, table, image and useful link.
- Record each as converted, merged into another section, or intentionally omitted
  with a reason. Exclude empty headings, navigation templates and duplicate pages.
- Preserve useful detail; compact layout is not permission to replace directories
  or mechanics with a few examples.
- Keep citations and contributor-history/license links. Mark unconverted detail
  links as external references; never create empty local articles for them.
- Copy article images and previews to R2 with source credits and verify their
  public URLs before publishing. Support images inside their relevant sections.
- Keep existing editable IDs and baseline text stable. If a baseline must change,
  reconcile its live revision first; never silently orphan community corrections.
- Retain the existing quest map and quest-specific YouTube verification policy.
- A source-reviewed badge means reviewed content, not exhaustive source coverage.
